# H6 survey methodology

## Source and scope

The September 11, 2026 Google Forms CSV contains 150 submitted rows covering awareness, prior consumption, purchase behavior, product preference, barriers, price expectations and farmer-support influence.

## Validation result

- Submitted rows: 150
- Exact duplicate rows: 102
- Unique timestamps: 29
- Unique answer patterns excluding timestamp: 48

The project owner chose to retain all submitted rows. FonioFlow therefore labels the denominator as **submitted records**, not independently verified respondents. Results describe this file and do not represent a population estimate.

## Calculation rules

Single-choice counts use normalized, trimmed answers. `yes` and `Yes` map to `Yes`, while `no` and `No` map to `No`. Multi-select answers are split into individual options. Every percentage uses 150 submitted records as its denominator, including questions with missing answers. Missing answers remain missing rather than becoming a response category.

## Main findings

- 37 submitted records reported prior awareness of fonio (24.7%).
- 10 reported having eaten fonio (6.7%).
- 80 reported that they were very or somewhat likely to purchase within three months at an acceptable price (53.3%).
- 95 reported that farmer-support information would make them much more or slightly more likely to purchase (63.3%).

These results support further market testing and awareness work. The duplication pattern prevents a population-level conclusion about consumer demand.
